Drive ten laps. Get a plan.

The Lap Coach listens to the same GT7 Live Connector as the telemetry dashboard. Nothing leaves your PC: the laps are kept in this page and analysed here. It finds the corners from your own line, measures what you do at each one lap after lap, and tells you where the time is and what to try next session.

1
Run GT7-Live-Connector (the same one the dashboard uses). In GT7: Settings → Assists → Send Vehicle Data → On.
2
Pick a track and a car, go to Time Trial or Practice, and drive. Every completed lap is counted below. Out-laps and laps where you leave the track are kept too; the analysis uses them all and tells you which was best.
3
After three laps the report builds itself, and it refreshes every time you cross the line after that. The target is just the number you are aiming for; keep going past it and the report keeps improving.
