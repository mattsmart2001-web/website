How it works
1
Run the connector. The same GT7-Live-Connector the telemetry dashboard uses, on the PC next to your console. If you already have it, you are ready. Already running SimHub, a motion rig or another lap timer? They can share the telemetry: right-click the connector's tray icon and choose Settings.
Download connector single-file .exe Mac (Apple Silicon) Linux (x86_64)
2
Drive. Pick a track and a car, go to Time Trial or Practice, and put some laps in. Scruffy laps and out-laps are kept as well; the report uses them all and tells you which was best.
3
Read the report. It builds itself after three laps and refreshes every time you cross the line. The target is only what you are aiming for, so keep going past it and it keeps improving.
For streamers

Put your lap times on your stream

This page can strip itself down to just the lap strip, on a transparent background, for OBS. It logs the laps itself, so it works with nothing else open, and the lap you are on counts up live. Set it up here and paste the address into a browser source.

PREVIEW
Open it

In OBS: add a Browser source, paste that in, set it to about 900 x 220, and untick Shutdown source when not visible so it keeps logging while you are on another scene. Transparent needs no keying. If you would rather capture the window and key it in your editor, choose green screen. Open this page normally in another tab at the same time and the overlay follows that session instead of starting its own, so your report and your overlay always agree.

MEMBERS: COMPARE AGAINST A FASTER DRIVER

Channel members can load a reference lap and have the report measure you against it corner by corner: where the time goes, who brakes later, who carries more speed, and what to change. Your code is in the members-only post on the channel.

Unlocked. GT7 sends telemetry while a replay plays, just as it does while you drive, so you can record a fast driver's lap with the connector you already have.

1
In GT7, open the leaderboard for the race or time trial you are driving and start a top ten driver's replay. Any of them will do; they are all a long way up the road.
2
Press Capture from a replay below, then let the replay run. The page waits for the car to cross the line and records the lap from there, so it does not matter where you join it.
3
It keeps the quickest complete lap it sees, and stops on its own after two. If the replay only holds one lap, press Stop when it has finished. Then drive your own laps and the report will measure you against theirs.

Where your laps differ

Time between your best and worst lap at each corner. Tall bars are corners you have not settled yet. Gold is the corner that opens onto the longest straight, where exit speed is worth most.

Lap against lap

Pick any two of your laps and see exactly where they parted company. Both are already laid over the same piece of road, so this compares what you did in the same place rather than at the same moment.

What to try next session

Ranked by how much time each one is likely worth. Everything here is measured from your own telemetry.

Every braking corner, pedal and numbers

Brake in blue and throttle in green against distance from the apex, your best lap bold and the others faint. A vertical wall is a stamp; a slope is a squeeze. The brake ending before the apex line is you off the pedal before the turn-in, and the gap before the green rises is coasting.
