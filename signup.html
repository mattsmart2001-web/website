Nordschleife 24 Hours

A solo GT7 endurance test around the Green Hell. One driver, one car, twenty-four hours. Sign up below — selected drivers will be announced on 12th June 2026.

Event Details

  • Race Day Friday 26 June 2026 — 12:00 noon BST start, finishing Saturday 27 June at 12:00 BST.
  • Track Nürburgring Nordschleife — 24-hour layout, all conditions.
  • Format Solo only. One driver per car, full 24 hours behind the wheel.
  • Selection No entry cap. The selected drivers will be announced on 12th June 2026.
  • Sign-Up Deadline Friday 12 June 2026, 23:59 BST — two weeks before lights out.
  • Comms Selected drivers will be contacted by email and on Discord with full briefing details.
