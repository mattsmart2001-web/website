Frequently Asked

Quick answers. Anything we've missed? DM @SparksTheory or ask in the Discord.

Do I really have to drive the full 24 hours non-stop?

Yes — this is a solo endurance event, one driver behind the wheel from lights out to the chequered flag. You can pause briefly for natural breaks, but the clock keeps running.

What happens if I get disconnected mid-race?

This is unfortunate and can happen. Unfortunately Gran Turismo doesn't allow rejoining an event after a disconnection.

Can I change my Gr.3 car after signing up?

Yes — just submit the form again with the updated car. Your most recent submission is the one that counts, so you can switch as many times as you like before the briefing.

Will the race be streamed?

Yes — full coverage live on the SparksTheory YouTube channel. Live viewers are also in the draw for the 10% Sim-Lab voucher.

How will I know if I've been selected?

The draw happens live on the SparksTheory YouTube stream on 12 June 2026 — tune in to see the grid come together. Selected drivers will also be contacted via the email address and Discord username on the form, so make sure both are accurate when you sign up.

How are the prizes delivered?

Sim-Lab arranges shipping with the winners directly after the event. The S1 Enduro GT Seat is the headline prize for 1st place; 2nd and 3rd receive discount vouchers usable on the full Sim-Lab catalogue.
