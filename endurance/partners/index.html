The Opportunity

Not a shout-out. A campaign.

GTEC pairs an established 38K-subscriber GT7 creator with a fully-branded championship: a recurring season of live-broadcast racing that draws 1,000+ combined concurrent viewers across the grid's streams, a driver grid of 162 across 10+ splits, and a purpose-built platform with standings, ratings and broadcast graphics.

The result is something a one-off influencer post can't offer: an ongoing, ownable property in front of a young, engaged gaming audience. The people who buy the hardware, and exactly who energy, coffee and lifestyle brands compete to reach, season after season, race after race.
