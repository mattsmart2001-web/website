Privacy Policy
GTEC Gran Turismo 7 Endurance Championship

This policy explains what personal data GTEC (the Gran Turismo 7 endurance championship, run by SparksTheory) collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It covers both the GTEC website and the GTEC Android app, which are the same service.

Who we are

GTEC is an amateur Gran Turismo 7 sim-racing league operated by SparksTheory. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@sparkstheory.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Account details: the email address and password you use to sign in (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us), and your display name.
  • Entry details: when you apply as a driver or team, the information you submit in the application form (such as your driver name, team name and chosen manufacturer).
  • Competition data: your race results, championship points, standings, penalties and any profile information you add. Most of this is published as part of running a public championship.
  • Messages: any messages you send to the organisers through the site.
  • Technical data: standard information your browser or device sends when loading the site, and data stored locally on your device to keep you signed in and let the app work offline.

How we use it

  • To run the championship: register entries, record results, calculate standings and publish them.
  • To let you sign in and manage your profile.
  • To contact you about the league, your application, or decisions that affect you.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We process your data to provide a service you have asked to take part in (performance of a contract), on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a fair and public championship, and, where relevant, with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Where your data is stored

The service is built on the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase - database, authentication and file storage.
  • Netlify - website and app hosting.
  • Google Play - distribution of the Android app.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr - delivery of fonts and code libraries used by the site.

These providers may process data outside the UK. We only use reputable providers with appropriate safeguards in place.

Cookies and local storage

We use local storage and similar technologies for essential purposes only: to keep you signed in and to cache pages so the app loads quickly and works offline. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and competition data for as long as you take part in GTEC and while it forms part of the championship record. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal data, though published historical results may be retained in anonymised or aggregated form as part of the league's records.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. To exercise any of these, contact privacy@sparkstheory.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Children

GTEC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date below shows when it was last changed, and material changes will be noted on the site.
