Delete Your Account
GTEC Gran Turismo 7 Endurance Championship

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for GTEC, the Gran Turismo 7 endurance championship run by SparksTheory (the same account is used on the GTEC website and the GTEC Android app).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to privacy@sparkstheory.co.uk from the email address on your GTEC account, so we can confirm it is you.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. Include your driver or display name so we can find your entry.

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion. If you are signed in, you can also reach us from My Profile.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your login and email address.
  • Your display name and driver or team details.
  • Your profile information.
  • Messages you have sent to the organisers.
  • Any images or media you have submitted.

What we may keep

Published historical race results, classifications and championship standings may be retained as part of the permanent competition record, in anonymised or aggregated form once your account is removed. We may also keep limited information where we are required to by law.

How long it takes

We action account deletion requests within 30 days of confirming them.

For any question about this process or your data, contact privacy@sparkstheory.co.uk. See also our Privacy Policy.
