
## Full description (max 4000)

```
GTEC is a team-based Gran Turismo 7 endurance championship run by SparksTheory. This is the official app: everything you need to follow the season, whether you are racing in it or watching it.

FOLLOW THE CHAMPIONSHIP
See the Drivers, Teams and Manufacturers standings update as results come in, with points, positions and season form all in one place.

NEVER MISS A ROUND
The calendar lists every round of the season in order, with a live countdown to the next race so you always know when lights go out.

RESULTS AND STEWARDING
Full round classifications alongside the steward decisions that shaped them, each with its incident reference, so you can find the ruling that affected you without reading the whole report.

DRIVERS AND TEAMS
Driver and team profiles with their campaign so far, results history and the details that matter, including each team's locked manufacturer.

STAY IN THE LOOP
Round reports, stewards' bulletins, calendar updates and championship news in one feed, plus a media area for liveries and race galleries.

RULES AND ENTRY
The full sporting regulations are always to hand, and new drivers and teams can apply straight from the app.

GTEC runs Gr.3 cars, teams of two, locked manufacturers and full stewarding across a season of endurance rounds. The app shows the same live data as the website, so results and standings are always current.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony or Polyphony Digital. Gran Turismo is a trademark of its respective owner.
```
