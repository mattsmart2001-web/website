Endurance racing, properly

A multi-season, Gr.3-only endurance championship. Teams of two to four. Cars locked to a manufacturer for the year. Every race a minimum of six hours.

The Format

Built like a real championship.

One class. Locked manufacturers. Real strategy. The format is simple by design — but every decision a team makes in the off-season shapes the season ahead.

01

Gr.3 Only

One class of car, no balance-of-performance arguments — drivers and teams choose their weapon, then live with it.

02

Teams of 2–4

Two-driver line-ups for a six-hour race, up to four for the longer rounds. Stints, fatigue, and strategy all count.

03

Locked Manufacturer

Each team registers a manufacturer for the season. Pick wisely — it sticks until the next championship.

04

Six Hours Minimum

Every round is real endurance. From six-hour sprints to the full 12 Hours of Le Mans on the calendar finale.
