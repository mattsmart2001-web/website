1 · Load

1b · Live (instead of a file)

Drive the overlay from the running replay in real time. Telemetry from the connector; video from a capture card / screen, or leave it off and use this page as a transparent OBS browser source over your game capture.
Or open ?obs=1 directly as the browser-source URL. It reuses the calibration and zones saved in this browser. The panel stays on screen and interactive so you can tune it live via OBS's Interact button - crop the source in your scene to keep the panel out of the actual broadcast.

2 · Camera calibration

Dial these until a red zone sits flat on the tarmac and stays put as you drive past it. Brake dive is separate: GT7's bumper cam cosmetically dips under braking (the horizon rises) which isn't in the telemetry at all, so raise it from 0 until objects stop sinking relative to the road under heavy braking.
Points the camera along the direction of travel instead of the raw yaw field. Fixes the overlay sliding sideways as you drive. Turn off only if you want to test the raw telemetry orientation.
Lower = steadier but laggier in corners; higher = snappier but jumpier.
Turn the grid on and calibrate until its lines lie flat on the road and stay put as you drive. A whole grid is far easier to line up than one box. It tilts with the current slope and heading, so it should climb on uphills and dip on downhills rather than staying dead level.

3 · Author a no-go zone

Scrub so the car sits at a corner of the penalty patch, then drop a point. 3+ points make a zone.
In edit mode: drag a gold handle to move a corner, the blue handle to move the whole box. Click a corner to select it, then arrow keys nudge it (Shift = bigger steps).

4 · Fun props

Drops a random 3D object on the track ahead, with a soft contact shadow so it sits on the road. They stick to the world like the zones, fade out past 200m so far-off parts of the track don't show through, and show in OBS mode too.

5 · Obstacles

Builds a brick wall across the track ahead. It shatters into individual bricks the moment the car reaches it, so it reads as smashing through.
A 10-pin rack close ahead. Tips over and scatters the moment the car reaches it.
A ball on a chain, swinging across the track ahead - persists and keeps swinging, no smash trigger. Time your pass and dodge it.
A staggered burst of colourful fireworks far down the track, high enough to stay visible from a distance - a celebration trigger, not an obstacle.
Removes every prop, wall, pin, wrecking ball and firework in one go - a full reset between takes. Leaves zones and calibration untouched.
For a Stream Deck: add a Website action set to GET a trigger URL on the machine running the connector - http://localhost:8767/trigger?type=wall for the wall, ...?type=pins for the pins, ...?type=wreckingball for the wrecking ball, ...?type=fireworks for fireworks, ...?type=clear to clear everything. Only reaches this page while connected to live telemetry (Connect telemetry, above); the test buttons here always work.

Notes

Deterministic replay + a rigid bumper cam is what makes this work: calibrate once, and the zones stay glued to the track. This is a fit test, not the finished overlay. Orientation offsets in the telemetry are unverified - if the world tilts, that is the pitch/roll field to confirm.
